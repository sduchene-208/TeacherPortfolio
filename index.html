I combine strong relationships with reflective practice.

I believe students learn best when they feel known, supported, and appropriately challenged. My work brings together classroom relationships, differentiated instruction, assessment evidence, and collaboration.

Across my elementary teaching roles, I have developed small-group reading interventions, strengthened family communication, collaborated on grade-level instruction, and informally supported newer teachers.
